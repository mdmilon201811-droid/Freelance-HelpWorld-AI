# Freelance HelpWorld AI — product scope

## Goal
An international freelance marketplace with AI assistance for clients and freelancers, project discovery, workflow support and advertiser workflows.

## Product principles
- Support clients and freelancers with clear profiles, project descriptions, proposals and work-status tracking.
- AI may assist with drafting, matching and analysis; users remain responsible for final submissions and agreements.
- Advertising must be reviewed for the laws and platform rules of each target country. Hold uncertain content for human review.
- Show fees and deductions transparently before a user confirms a paid transaction.
- Do not collect application fees or promise guaranteed work.
- Protect user accounts, private messages, identity documents and payment information.

## Not implemented
Production marketplace, escrow, dispute resolution, payouts, AI integrations, KYC and country-specific compliance are not complete.
